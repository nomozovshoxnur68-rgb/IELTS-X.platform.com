FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Sign in and use Report Issue inside any test.

Is IELTS-X free?

Yes, you can create an account and take the free tests without paying. The full library, the mock tests and AI feedback come with a Standard, Max or Ultra plan.

How is my band worked out?

Listening and Reading are marked again against the answer key on our server, so the band you see is the band that is saved. Writing and Speaking get a band and feedback on each of the four criteria from our AI examiner.

What is the difference between Real exam and Practice mode?

In Real exam mode there is no submit button: each paper ends when its time is up, like on test day. In Practice mode you can submit each paper yourself before the time runs out.

Can I use it on my phone?

The practice pages work on a phone. For the full mock test use a computer: it runs in full screen, the way the computer-delivered exam does.

Will my results be saved?

Yes. Every band is saved to your account and shows on your Performance page, so you can see how you improve.
